nfc_url_tag

Hello from an NFC tag

If you got here by holding your phone to another one, that Android phone was the tag. It shared this link over NFC. You didn’t need an app to read it.

Get the Flutter plugin Source

What just happened

  1. A Flutter app called startSharing with this page’s address.
  2. The phone started answering NFC readers as a Type 4 tag.
  3. Your phone read the link and offered to open it.

The whole integration

await NfcUrlTag.instance.startSharing(
  url: 'https://azati1.github.io/nfc_url_tag/',
);
Android only: iOS doesn’t let third-party apps emulate NFC tags. MIT licensed.
